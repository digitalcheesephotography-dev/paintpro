Manual Entry & Debug
🎤 Voice Commands

Tap the mic 🎤 then say one of these. Most commands work with or without "add" / "put" / "set" in front.

Measurements
"wall fourteen" — fills next wall (14 ft)
"wall twelve five" — 12 ft 5 in
"height nine" — ceiling height
"length twenty" — floor/ceiling length
"width sixteen" — floor/ceiling width
Doors & Windows
"six doors" or "doors six"
"add seventeen doors"
"eight windows" or "windows eight"
"put twelve windows"
Switch Tabs
"estimator" or "measure"
"clients" or "contacts"
"projects"
"materials" or "shopping list"
"notes"
"apt pricing" or "apartment"
Add New
"add room" or "new room"
"add client" or "new contact"
"add project"
"add note"
"add materials" or "new list"
Actions
"generate bid" or "proposal"
"connect" or "bluetooth"
💡 Numbers can be spoken ("seventeen") or said as digits ("17"). For wall fractions, say the inches: "wall twelve five" = 12'5".
